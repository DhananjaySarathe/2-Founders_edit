# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Founders and small teams (startups and small businesses, in India and abroad) who need a product, internal tool or AI workflow built and would rather hire a small contract team than staff up. They arrive from a referral, a cold link or a project page, and want to judge quickly whether this team has shipped real things like what they need, and how to start.

## Product Purpose

The marketing site for 2 Founders: a two-person development team that works on contract, freelance and fixed-scope projects. It exists to show shipped work convincingly and turn a visitor into a written brief or an email to hello@2founders.space. Success is a qualified enquiry that names a concrete task.

## Positioning

Two developers with 3+ years of freelance and contract work who pivoted fully to AI-first building: they ship working products (desktop apps, web apps, Chrome extensions, AI editors, automation workflows) rather than decks, and they built several of these tools for themselves first. The founders are not named anywhere on the site.

## Operating Context

- Engagements are contract, freelance or fixed-scope project work, plus ongoing AI workflow optimisation.
- Contact is email: hello@2founders.space. The enquiry form prepares an email in the visitor's own mail app; nothing is stored on the site.
- Static HTML/CSS/JS deployed on Vercel at https://2founders.space/, with a small `/api/geo` edge function.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Shipped work the site can show (descriptions limited to what each project's own page or the owner states):

- **Framecast**: free, open-source macOS live studio. Streams and records a 16:9 and a 9:16 canvas at once; an AI director follows what you say and switches layouts and graphics; one shortcut undoes a switch. Built for their own content creation, then open-sourced. https://dhananjaysarathe.github.io/framecast-site/
- **AI video editor** (client work, private): a Claude-based editor. The team analysed the client's existing videos and reels and built custom skills so the editor produces consistent, on-style edits for that client's content. No link, no code, no client name.
- **ExpenseWaale**: shared-expense app for flatmates, trips and teams; spaces, split by percentage/shares/equally, minimum-transaction settlements, PDF/share reports. Built as a direct competitor to an established bill-splitting app (competitor not named). https://expensewalle.vercel.app/
- **StockTax Pro** (client project): Indian capital-gains tax tool. Imports trades from Angel One, Kite and Upstox; shows STCG, LTCG and F&O; finds tax-loss harvesting opportunities to set losses off against gains before March 31. https://stock-tax-pro.vercel.app/landing
- **Liquid Signal**: Chrome extension that turns sampled public YouTube comments into audience insights (questions with 2–6 answers, answer splits, summary, CSV/JSON export). https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/liquid-signal-d1/bcopabbcdihpdoichjlfgmbllfloicki
- **n8n personal assistant agent** (client project, US): an n8n AI agent behind a Streamlit chat that handles Google Calendar, Gmail, Tasks, Docs, Sheets expenses and web search; built before off-the-shelf AI agents could do this. https://sartha555k.github.io/n8n-ai-agent-walkthrough/

Open: WhatsApp and booking links (none yet).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: 2 Founders. Domain 2founders.space. Email hello@2founders.space.
- Keep the existing colour theme (cream ground, lime, coral, near-black) and its overall feel; the owner pinned this.
- Present as a team of two; never name the founders.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live project pages listed above, the owned 48s editing sample `media/pitch.mp4` and poster, and the @2Founders YouTube channel.
- No testimonials, client logos, user counts, ratings, revenue, accuracy or savings figures may be shown. ExpenseWaale's and StockTax Pro's own marketing numbers are not to be repeated.
- Do not claim Framecast is "better than" OBS or Streamlabs; describe what it does differently.

## Product Principles

- Show the shipped thing, link it live, say what problem it solved.
- Plain, specific language a founder can act on; no hype, no invented numbers.
- One small team, close to the work: the visitor talks to the people who build.
- Every path ends in a concrete brief.
