  2. AI Video Editor

    Client build · private

    The brief
    A creator wanted every edit to look like theirs, without briefing an editor from scratch each time.
    What we built
    We studied their videos and reels, wrote how they cut, caption and pace into custom Claude skills, and built an editor around them that edits in their style.

    Claude-based AI editor · built for a content creator

    The code and screens stay with the client. Ask us and we'll walk you through how it works.

    Live web app

    Build a challenger to the bill-splitting app everyone already uses.

    Open ExpenseWaale

  4. StockTax Pro

    The brief
    A client needed Indian traders to see what tax they owe, and what they can still offset before March 31.

How hiring us works.

  1. A fixed-scope project

    One product or tool, scoped and priced before we start. You get the working thing, the code and a proper handover.

  2. A contract

    We work alongside your team on what's next on your roadmap, for as long as the work needs, on terms we agree upfront.

  3. An AI workflow tune-up

    We find the work your team keeps repeating and build the AI workflow that takes it over, inside the tools you already use.

  1. You send the brief

    A few lines on what you need is enough. We read it ourselves and come back with questions.

  2. Scope, price, timeline

    You get all three in writing, and nothing starts until you agree to them.

  3. We build

    You see working software along the way, not slides, and you can change course early.

  4. Handover

    You get the code, the deployment and a walkthrough. If you want us to keep going, that becomes a contract.

Questions founders ask us

Yes. We've built for a client in the US, and we work remotely, so your time zone isn't a problem.

A fixed-scope project gets one fixed price, agreed before we start. A contract runs on terms we agree upfront. Either way, you get a quote once we've read your brief.

Yes. On a contract we work in your repository, your tools and your process, alongside the people you already have.

What happens after launch?

You own what we hand over: the code, the deployment and the notes on how it works. If you'd like us to keep improving it, we can do that on a contract.
