Questions before we start?

What do you mean by an agentic workflow?

A connected process where AI reads an input and takes the next agreed steps across your tools, instead of stopping at an answer in a chat. We design the steps, limits, and review points around the job.

Do I need to know exactly what to automate?

No. Bring the repetitive task, the tools you use, and the result you want. We'll help work out whether automation makes sense and where to start.

Is this only for YouTube creators?

No. The creator engine and Liquid Signal are examples of what we've built. We build automation for founders and teams too, so bring us your process.

Can I use just one service?

Yes. Ask for a channel audit, content ideas, audience research, or AI editing on its own, as one-off, recurring, or usage-based work.

How do pricing and timelines work?

They depend on the workflow, the tools involved, volume, and support. We agree the scope, deliverables, price, and timeline before starting.

Does AI publish or send anything without my approval?

Only if you agree it should. We set the review points upfront, including where you check content or outputs before anything is published or sent.
