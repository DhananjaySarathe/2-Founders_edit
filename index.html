A few things you might be wondering

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Have a particular process in mind? Tell us about it.

What do you mean by an agentic workflow?

A connected process where AI can interpret an input and take the next agreed steps across tools, instead of stopping at an answer in a chat. We design the steps, boundaries, and review points around the job you need done.

Do I need to know exactly what to automate?

No. Bring the repetitive task, the tools you use, and the result you want. We'll help work out whether automation makes sense and where to start.

Can I use just one service?

Yes. You can ask for a channel audit, content ideas, audience research, or AI-assisted editing on its own. We can discuss one-off, recurring, and usage-based delivery based on the work.

Is this only for YouTube creators?

No. The creator engine and Liquid Signal are examples of what we've built. We also take on custom automation for founders and teams. Tell us your process so we can assess the fit.

How do pricing and timelines work?

They depend on the workflow, connected tools, volume, and support needed. We agree the scope, deliverables, price, and timeline before starting. Send a short brief and we'll work through the right approach.

Does AI publish or send things without my approval?

That depends on the workflow we agree with you. We define approval points upfront, including where you review content or outputs before they are published or sent.
