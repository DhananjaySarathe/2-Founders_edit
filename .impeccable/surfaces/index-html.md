## Scope

Homepage (`index.html`), the whole site. Visitor mode: Persuade (a founder decides whether to hire this team and sends a brief).

Audience and action: founders and small teams; read the shipped work, believe two people can carry their build, email hello@2founders.space or send the enquiry form. Proof: six shipped projects with live links (one private client project shown by description only). No numbers, testimonials or logos.

Constraints: keep the incumbent palette (cream #faf8f4, ink #141414, coral #e8552c, lime #c9f24b) and the self-hosted Inter / Inter Tight / JetBrains Mono. Founders never named. Static HTML/CSS/JS on Vercel.

Pinned references (owner): dakshgajjar.com for the scroll-drawn thread and giant type, neutomni.com for the small availability details (open-for-projects status, local time, full-width wordmark).

## Direction contract

THESIS: One coral thread draws itself down the page as you scroll and runs through every project we shipped, so the visitor literally follows the work from brief to handover. Refuses the services-grid agency page with cards and logos.

OWN-WORLD: Cream paper ground alternating with full-bleed ink fields; ink display type in Inter Tight 800 at poster scale; a 2px coral thread with a ringed tip; hairline ink line-drawings for each project; lime only for live/available states, the primary action, and the one marker block behind "A TEAM" carried over from the incumbent page's lime highlight (owner asked to keep the theme).

STORY: Two developers ship whole products on contract. Here are six they shipped and what each solved. Send us the task.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (wordmark left, Work / How we work / Contact, lime-dot "Open for projects" + IST clock, "Start a project" button). Headline "We ship what you'd hire a team for." fills ~85% width over three lines, the thread entering top-left, looping through the letters (passing in front and behind) and leaving down-right with its tip. Bottom row: one-sentence offer left, "Follow the thread" cue right.

FORM: Pinned by the owner (Daksh thread + Neutomni details) inside the established 2 Founders world; the roll ran and the owner's pin outranks it. seed:fb443d67f6087081

## Signature interaction and motion

The thread: one SVG path through anchor points in each section, stroke drawn to the scroll position with a ringed tip riding its end; front-layer crossings over the hero letters; forks into three branches at the capabilities statement. Line drawings ink in as their chapter arrives. Reduced motion: thread fully drawn, no scrub.

## Phones

The header keeps the wordmark and "Start a project"; the open-for-projects status and IST clock move to the footer because three items do not fit at 390px. The thread keeps to an inset right rail and swoops only into the giant titles; loops and front crossings are skipped. Drawings bleed to the left edge so their labels render at 11px or more.
