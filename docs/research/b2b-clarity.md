# B2B software clarity research for 2 Founders

Retrieved on 11 October 2026. This is a primary-source text and information-architecture review, not visual inspection: no claims below depend on seeing layout, animation, color, or viewport sizing. Headlines are paraphrased; CTA labels are short transcriptions. Product demonstrations are identified from retrieved text, interface content, or image descriptions. Numbers and testimonials are examples of each company's proof pattern, not independently validated performance claims and not claims 2 Founders can reuse.

Records 1–50 cover 50 successfully retrieved, distinct B2B software websites. Sentry's homepage returned an unsupported content-type error and was excluded; Help Scout replaces it. Brevo's homepage and marketing-platform page exposed only an analytics iframe and were excluded; Braze replaces it. Redirects are identified where relevant. The lessons are design judgments for 2 Founders: broad business automation first, custom builds and focused services as separate engagement routes, and Liquid Signal / creator workflows as existing evidence.

## Five syntheses for the redesign

1. **The first message needs a category, a buyer, and a delivery noun.** [Linear](https://linear.app/), [Attio](https://attio.com/), [Supabase](https://supabase.com/), and [Dropbox](https://www.dropbox.com/) clarify their more expressive headlines with concrete descriptions. For 2 Founders, a fixed AI-automation headline plus a sentence naming custom workflows and focused services makes the offer easier to understand than an identity built only around creator work.

2. **Make the engagement choice visible before the catalog.** [Stripe](https://stripe.com/en-pl) and [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) explicitly allow individual components or the connected whole; [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/) quickly explains two related products. For 2 Founders, custom automation and one-off/recurring/usage-based services should be visible beside or immediately below the hero, with actions that prepare the matching enquiry.

3. **Demonstrate one complete, legible job.** [Calendly](https://calendly.com/), [Linear](https://linear.app/), and [Amplitude](https://amplitude.com/) connect an input to useful work and an output. For 2 Founders, lead with a general business example such as brief → organized information → human review → task and draft reply. Mark it illustrative, following the transparency pattern retrieved from [PostHog](https://posthog.com/). Keep audience research and creator workflows as alternate, evidenced examples.

4. **Give every section one clear question to answer.** [Notion](https://www.notion.com/), [Dropbox](https://www.dropbox.com/), and [Webflow](https://webflow.com/) structure their narratives around tasks rather than uninterrupted feature inventory. For 2 Founders: what can you automate → how can we engage → what have you built → how does delivery work → how do we start. Use the existing cream, lime, coral, typography, borders, and animation style to distinguish these sections; this research does not justify replacing the brand's theme.

5. **Use evidence that matches the claim.** [Retool](https://retool.com/), [Help Scout](https://www.helpscout.com/), and [Gong](https://www.gong.io/) attach customer proof to specific work. 2 Founders can show Liquid Signal, the channel-audit/ideas/edit workflow described by the owner, and existing editing outputs. Do not invent customer logos, savings statistics, deployment scale, integration partnerships, certifications, or case-study results to resemble these larger companies.
