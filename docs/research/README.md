# Research behind the automation-first landing page

Reviewed 11 October 2026. The survey covers **200 distinct primary websites** across four groups of 50. Redirects count once under the canonical destination. Failed, blocked, contentless, and duplicate sites were excluded or replaced. Netguru appears in the automation-services group; REJOUICE replaces it in the studio group.

The research inspected retrieved page text, navigation, CTA labels, section order, example artifacts, buying models, and published proof. It did **not** visually inspect 200 rendered websites or establish conversion performance. Individual notes identify limited extractions and changed products. The actual 2 Founders implementation was separately reviewed in Chromium at desktop, tablet, and mobile sizes.

## Evidence boundaries

- Liquid Signal is the shipped Chrome extension. Its audience report uses sampled public YouTube comments; it is separate from full channel audits and the creator engine.
- The creator engine is described using the owner's stated workflow: channel audit → three daily ideas → recording → AI editing and review. Existing owned videos show editing output; they are not presented as measured end-to-end client case studies.
- Broader research/reporting and admin workflows are potential custom-build scopes, not claimed prebuilt products or deployed client systems.
- Competitor client logos, savings figures, certifications, prices, guarantees, and security claims were not transferred to 2 Founders.
- The palette, local fonts, original motion motifs, and owned video library remain. These changes aim to make the offer easier to understand; no conversion uplift is claimed.

## Experience pass (11 October 2026)

The owner's brief for this pass: keep the theme, colours, and feel, but make the page an experience that explains who 2 Founders are and what they do to someone arriving cold. Public sources were checked first and returned nothing (the YouTube channel, Chrome Web Store listing, and domain are not indexed or reachable from the build environment). The repository is therefore the only evidence: the current and previous site copy, the git history (the original “You focus on creating. We edit.” pitch site), the owned videos, and the 48-second pitch video in `media/pitch.mp4`.

| Gap on the previous page                                               | Decision                                                                                                                                                 | Evidence used                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| No one was behind the work; the name “2 Founders” was never explained. | Add a “Who we are” chapter, and name the two founders in the hero sentence.                                                                              | Original site copy (“Two founders who…”), the @2Founders channel, owned videos.                                    |
| The studio's point of view was missing.                                | Centre the story on the pitch video's own framing: Speed (AI-powered workflow) + Taste (human creativity, here “human judgment”), “We combined both.”    | Frames at 0:20–0:25 of `media/pitch.mp4`.                                                                          |
| The workflow board was a diagram of four labels.                       | Give every stage a small artifact so the input, intermediate work, review, and output are visible, and play the board when it is on screen.              | Lindy, Gumloop, and Bardeen patterns above: show input → steps → review → result.                                  |
| Visitors had to translate the offer to their own work.                 | Add the week meter: visitors pick their repeat work, see it in a 40-hour week, and send the list as a brief.                                             | “Help visitors locate their repeated task without demanding that they already know which service category to buy.” |
| Trust signals were thin and the FAQ was hidden in a drawer.            | State four working principles (one task first, your tools stay, you keep the calls, no surprises). Show the FAQ, and say what happens after the enquiry. | Existing FAQ and process copy; earlier v1 FAQ answers.                                                             |
| No guidance for a first-time visitor.                                  | Add a chapter guide and an optional 60-second tour, both dismissible, keyboard-accessible, and reduced-motion aware.                                     | Owner request for “some kind of small guide”.                                                                      |

Boundaries kept from earlier passes: no client logos, testimonials, savings figures, prices, timelines, or guarantees; illustrative interfaces stay labelled; the meter does arithmetic on the visitor's own numbers and makes no claim about hours saved.
