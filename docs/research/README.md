# Research behind the automation-first landing page

Reviewed 11 October 2026. The survey covers **200 distinct primary websites** across four groups of 50. Redirects count once under the canonical destination. Failed, blocked, contentless, and duplicate sites were excluded or replaced. Netguru appears in the automation-services group; REJOUICE replaces it in the studio group.

The research inspected retrieved page text, navigation, CTA labels, section order, example artifacts, buying models, and published proof. It did **not** visually inspect 200 rendered websites or establish conversion performance. Individual notes identify limited extractions and changed products. The actual 2 Founders implementation was separately reviewed in Chromium at desktop, tablet, and mobile sizes.

## What changed because of the research

| Previous problem | Applied decision | Representative primary references |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The opening slogan required visitors to infer what the company sells. | State “AI automation” in a fixed main headline. Supporting copy identifies founders, creators, and teams and names custom workflows and focused services. | [Osher](https://osher.com.au/), [n8n](https://n8n.io/), [Linear](https://linear.app/) |
| The first concrete demonstration was a creator engine, reinforcing the old editing identity. | Default to an explicitly illustrative business request → organised details → review → next-action workflow, with a named output. Audience research and the creator engine remain selectable examples. | [Lindy](https://www.lindy.ai/), [Gumloop](https://www.gumloop.com/), [PostHog](https://posthog.com/) |
| Broader capabilities appeared as small pills after the buying cards. | Give research/reporting, admin/handoffs, and creator workflows substantial cards with specific inputs and outputs. Add an invitation for other processes. | [InData Labs](https://indatalabs.com/), [Airtable](https://www.airtable.com/), [Makeitfuture](https://www.makeitfuture.com/) |
| The two buying models were easy to miss. | Show custom-build and service CTAs in the hero, then contrasting cards explaining what each engagement delivers. State one-off, recurring, and usage-based service options. | [Makeitfuture](https://www.makeitfuture.com/), [Clay](https://clay.global/), [Apify](https://apify.com/) |
| Shipped evidence was far below the first screen. | Add Liquid Signal and creator-engine links near the promise. On smaller screens, put this proof strip before the longer demonstration. | [Markovate](https://markovate.com/), [thoughtbot](https://thoughtbot.com/), [Work & Co](https://work.co/) |
| Long cream sections had similar visual weight. | Give the main sections numbered chapter markers, concise buyer-focused headings, stronger card hierarchy, and white/lime-soft section surfaces drawn from the existing palette. | The studio survey's service/work/engagement distinctions informed the structure. Actual visual treatments were designed and tested locally, not inferred from extracted competitor pages. |
| The illustrative workweek added length without explaining the offer. | Remove that demonstration and concentrate attention on actual capabilities, buying routes, and shipped work. | Editorial decision based on the repeated task → deliverable → evidence pattern. |

## Evidence boundaries

- Liquid Signal is the shipped Chrome extension. Its audience report uses sampled public YouTube comments; it is separate from full channel audits and the creator engine.
- The creator engine is described using the owner's stated workflow: channel audit → three daily ideas → recording → AI editing and review. Existing owned videos show editing output; they are not presented as measured end-to-end client case studies.
- Broader research/reporting and admin workflows are potential custom-build scopes, not claimed prebuilt products or deployed client systems.
- Competitor client logos, savings figures, certifications, prices, guarantees, and security claims were not transferred to 2 Founders.
- The palette, local fonts, original motion motifs, and owned video library remain. These changes aim to make the offer easier to understand; no conversion uplift is claimed.
