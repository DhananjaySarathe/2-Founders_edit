# AI automation services: primary-site research

Reviewed 11 October 2026: **50 unique primary sites successfully retrieved**. This group covers service providers, automation consultancies, and AI implementation firms. Each counted entry received a meaningful primary-page retrieval through the web tool. Notes describe page text, navigation, and extracted section order; they do **not** claim visually verified layouts, animation, colour, or screenshots. Reported customer results, credentials, and guarantees are the provider's own claims, recorded as proof patterns rather than independently verified facts or claims available to 2 Founders. Short quotations identify the actual offer/CTA; other wording is paraphrased. Examples and buying routes are editorial lessons, not evidence of conversion uplift.

## Five synthesis lessons for 2 Founders

1. **Establish the broad service business in the first screen.** Tensorway, Luhhu, BotsCrew, and Osher explicitly name custom work, operational workflows, or done-for-you implementation. A hero should explain that 2 Founders builds agents and automations for the customer's work, while retaining the existing cream/ink/lime/coral theme.
2. **Give the two buying routes equal clarity.** Makeitfuture, Azumo, Vention, and Zfort explain how to engage separately from what they build. 2 Founders should offer a custom automation project and a focused or usage-based service, describing scope without inventing prices, SLAs, or guarantees.
3. **Show business tasks before technical categories.** InData Labs, ThirdEye Data, Accelirate, and Osher help visitors navigate by outcome or function. Use examples such as research/reporting, lead or customer operations, document/admin workflows, and content production where actually offered; agents, integrations, and tools explain the mechanism.
4. **Keep shipped proof concrete and proportionate.** Markovate, Neoteric, Softweb, and Satalia attach proof to named tasks. Liquid Signal and the creator audit → ideas → edit workflow belong under a built-work/examples section. Label sample dashboards as illustrative and reserve quantified claims for measured 2 Founders evidence.
5. **Explain the path and ongoing responsibility.** HatchWorks, BotsCrew, Makeitfuture, and Tredence cover discovery, implementation, adoption, and continued support. A short map → build → test/review → run sequence will clarify delivery better than a dense AI-stack diagram. Describe only support, access, and human-review practices the company can actually provide.
