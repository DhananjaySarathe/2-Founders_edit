# Service studio research — Group D

Primary-page text retrieval for the second 2 Founders design pass. This is research into offer clarity, calls to action, engagement choices, and evidence structure; it is **not visual inspection**. “Entry offer” means the lead offer found in the extracted page text, not a verified screen position. No claim about colors, typography, animation, spacing, or actual viewport fold is made here. Metrics and client statements below are the studios' own published claims, not independently validated results. Lessons are our interpretation for 2 Founders.

**Completed sample: 50 unique studio websites with substantive primary retrievals; 55 candidates attempted, four retrievals excluded, and one successful retrieval assigned to Group B to avoid duplication.** Studio records use final primary URLs where the original domain redirected.

Count only sites whose primary pages returned substantive offer, service, or work information. Redirects count as one studio. Supplemental pages do not add to the site count. Blocked or contentless pages will be excluded and replaced.

## Five synthesis findings for 2 Founders

1. **Define the category before asking visitors to interpret a demo.** Clay, Instrument, Ramotion, Eleken, and Cuberto say what kind of company they are. Keep the existing cream/lime/coral identity, but make the broad AI-automation offer explicit in the first-view copy. A creator-specific workflow should support the offer later.
2. **Separate capabilities, engagement, and owned products.** Halo Lab, Fantasy, DEPT, and thoughtbot expose these as different questions. 2 Founders needs one unmistakable choice between a custom workflow build and focused/ongoing/usage-based services, with Liquid Signal presented as a real shipped product rather than another hiring model.
3. **Show real work near the claim and describe what it proves.** Work & Co, thoughtbot, Afternow, and AKQA connect proof to concrete work. Liquid Signal demonstrates product-building ability; the creator pipeline demonstrates a content automation application. Illustrative diagrams must remain labeled as examples.
4. **Make each section answer a different buyer question.** The retrieved studios distinguish work, capabilities, process, engagement, and company evidence with concrete headings. Our design inference is to give sections stronger chapter transitions and different internal structures within the current palette, rather than repeating equally weighted text-plus-card rows.
5. **Tell buyers what they receive and how to begin.** Lazarev names outcomes/deliverables; Eleken names buyer situations; Designjoy and Design Pickle explain delivery boundaries; Made Thought distinguishes questions from briefs. 2 Founders should combine a clear deliverable example, the appropriate engagement route, and an honest next step without inventing fixed prices, savings, or guaranteed outcomes.
